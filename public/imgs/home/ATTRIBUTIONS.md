# Homepage media

The photographs below show actual locations in Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan.
They were resized, encoded as WebP, and may be cropped responsively in the page.
CC-licensed image derivatives retain the source image license. The licensed
media do not imply any photographer's endorsement of the travel operator.

## Current homepage photographs

The current photographs are stored in `../home-premium/`. Sources were verified
at their native dimensions; none of these exports has been enlarged. The images
were resized without changing their aspect ratio and encoded as WebP at quality
90–92. The original photographer's edits, including the monochrome Passu image,
have been retained. Display crops may apply in the page. CC image derivatives
retain their individual source licenses; this does not relicense the website code.
